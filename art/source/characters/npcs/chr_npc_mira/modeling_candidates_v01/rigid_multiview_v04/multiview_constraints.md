# Mira v04 rigid multiview repair contract

Status: `MODELING CANDIDATE CONSTRAINT / NOT SOURCE LOCKED`

## Canonical policy

Policy: `front_side_canonical`.

- The accepted v03 front view supplies the head's X/Z silhouette and establishes that
  Mira's head is a two-layer rigid shell.
- The repaired exact profiles supply Y depth and must expose the same two rigid layers.
- The repaired back must be the rear projection of that same shell; it may not invent a
  central purple head layer or use shoulder/back plates as head geometry.
- Contradictory silhouettes are not averaged and view-specific cheats are forbidden.

The original 2D concept remains unchanged. This contract applies only to the repaired
realistic modeling candidate.

## Rigid two-layer head volume

The head is one asymmetric, swept-back, seed-pod-like rigid volume with two nested coral/
cream layers above the short purple neck notch:

1. **Upper/crown shell** — coral outer crown and brow shell. It owns the broad front top
   silhouette, slopes backward in profile and retains substantial Y depth; it must not
   collapse into a thin blade.
2. **Lower recessed shell** — a separately readable coral lower face/underside shell whose
   cream rim traces the interface under the upper crown. In front it is visibly inset under
   the brow. In both exact profiles the cream rim must continue from the muzzle/front edge
   toward the rear and enclose a visible recessed lower volume. It may narrow by occlusion,
   but it may not disappear or become a painted line.

The cavity between these layers is a real undercut/recess with thickness and self-occlusion.
The amber eye sits within the lower recessed shell under the crown overhang.

## View-specific head projections

- **Front:** retain v03's accepted upper overhang, inset lower face, cream interface rim,
  eye placement and asymmetric seed-pod silhouette.
- **Character-left profile:** increase the head's front-to-back depth; show upper crown
  thickness, the recessed lower shell below it, and a visible cream interface rim running
  toward the rear. One near eye only.
- **Back:** show the upper coral crown and the lower recessed coral/cream shell as matching
  left/right rear lobes. Their height, width and vertical registration must correspond to
  what exits both side profiles. The short purple neck notch begins below them. The central
  purple vertical/chevron stack belongs to the shoulder/back system and must begin below
  the head; it is not a third head layer. No facial features.
- **Character-right profile:** the same rigid layer thickness and depth as character-left,
  with the source-locked asymmetry preserved rather than mirrored. One near eye only.

## Tool pouch ownership and occlusion

The tool pouch is attached only to Mira's **physical left hip**. It is not a paired part.

- Front: visible on image-right, which is Mira's physical left.
- Character-left: visible as the near-side pouch.
- Back: visible on image-left, which is Mira's physical left from behind.
- Character-right: completely hidden behind the torso/physical-left silhouette; no pouch,
  pouch opening, purple tools, orange stitches or pouch edge may be visible.

The crossbody harness remains visible according to its path and must not be removed merely
because the pouch is occluded.
