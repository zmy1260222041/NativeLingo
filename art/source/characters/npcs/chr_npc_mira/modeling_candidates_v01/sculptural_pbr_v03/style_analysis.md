# Mira v03 style analysis

Status: `MODELING CANDIDATE DIRECTION / NOT SOURCE LOCKED`

## Visual identity

**Soft-Studio Sculpted Stylized-PBR Turnaround / 柔光雕塑式半写实 PBR 模型页**

The target should read first as a resolved digital character sculpture under a neutral
studio material-preview rig. Stylization remains in Mira's locked proportions and shape
language; realism comes from continuous three-dimensional curvature, real part thickness,
credible junctions, material-specific PBR response and restrained micro-surface variation.
It must not read as a line drawing with extra gradients.

The five supplied full-body turnarounds are style-only references. They do not authorize
copying anatomy, proportions, faces, head appendages, garments, palettes, markings or
accessories. Mira's `source_locked_v06/reference/turnaround_source.png` remains the sole
identity and design authority.

## Observable rules distilled from the references

1. Primary, secondary and tertiary forms are expressed as a continuous digital sculpture,
   not by inked contours or cel-shadow shapes.
2. Shoulders, arm roots, torso-to-pelvis transition, knees, ankles, palms and feet have
   readable attachment and bending structure in the complete full-body view.
3. Part boundaries have thickness, bevel, underside depth, overlap and compressed contact
   edges instead of a painted seam alone.
4. Studio shading is continuous and high-key, with open midtones, soft self-shadowing and
   character-local cavity occlusion.
5. Each material owns a different roughness, specular-lobe width, subtle normal relief and
   low-amplitude albedo variation.
6. Micro-surface exists but remains subordinate to the large form; it never becomes random
   noise, damage or a new design marking.
7. Eyes sit inside a volumetric socket with readable rim thickness and insertion depth.
8. Harness, clasp and pouch behave as independent thick objects that compress, overlap and
   tension against the body.
9. Highlights are restrained but complete; they are not globally suppressed.
10. Front, profile and back views describe the same sculpture, material definition and
    depth envelope.
11. Outer silhouettes are crisp while internal curvature transitions remain soft.
12. Hands, feet, joints and surface scale remain inspectable at full-body framing.

## What is borrowed

- High-resolution digital-sculpt finish and continuous volume.
- Physically coherent stylized PBR response under broad neutral studio lighting.
- Material-specific roughness, highlights, weave/grain and low-frequency variation.
- Readable eye recess, shell edge thickness, joints and equipment overlaps.
- A single stable three-dimensional character shared across orthographic views.

## What is explicitly excluded

- Any reference character's humanoid anatomy, five-digit hands, feet, facial construction,
  head crest, costume, palette, motif, marking or prop.
- Tall/slender human proportions, human clavicles, chest, waist, pelvis, buttocks, jaw,
  nose, lips, ears or photoreal human skin.
- Horizontal height guides, grids, rulers, panel separators or labels.
- Foot baseline, ground line, floor, horizon, platform, pedestal, cast/contact/drop shadow,
  ground ambient-occlusion stain or reflection.
- Random speckling, wear, scratches or texture that invents new Mira markings.

## Key correction from v02

v02 incorrectly translated “not photorealistic” into surfaces that were uniformly quiet,
matte, low-highlight and limited to large-scale separation. v03 instead permits and
requires controlled PBR richness: material-specific roughness/specular/normal response,
subtle coherent microrelief, directional harness weave and low-frequency tonal variation.
Only identity drift, humanization and noisy or design-changing realism remain prohibited.
