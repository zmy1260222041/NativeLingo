## Positive direction

Create exactly four orthographic views of the same source-locked Mira in front,
character-left, back and character-right order. Produce both a neutral A-pose and a
strict T-pose. Keep equal height, zero perspective, full feet and hands, and a perfectly
uniform solid neutral light-gray background.

Preserve Mira's compact 4.6–4.8-head nonhuman worker proportions, asymmetric swept
seed-pod head, short neck notch, trapezoid torso, separate pelvis core, reverse-curved
lower legs, split wedge feet, exact palette and physical handedness. Preserve exactly
three distinct interlocking shoulder plates, three digits per hand, the woven crossbody
harness, amber clasp, tool pouch, two repair-stitch groups, and exactly three plum ovals
on each outer upper arm with no ovals elsewhere.

Rendering language:

> polished 3D-painted production concept art; restrained stylized PBR material cues;
> soft-edged block-plane cartoon 3D; smooth organic planar nonhuman anatomy; default to
> no thick black outline. Use watertight-looking coherent geometry, real plate thickness,
> overlaps, joint insertions, broad matte roughness and restrained highlights.

Render the original eyes as deep-set inside shallow nonhuman head-shell sockets. Give
the existing lower seed shell a tapered jaw-like planar termination without adding a
human chin, jaw, nose, lips, ears or cheekbones. Make clavicle/scapula/pelvis/knee
readability come only from Mira's existing three shoulder plates and arm roots, pelvis
core and leg sockets, and angular knee hinge planes. Do not add literal human anatomy.

For the T-pose, front and back arms are horizontal with straight elbows, neutral wrists,
downward palms and three separated digits. In exact profiles the arms point along the
camera axis and appear end-on; do not lower or diagonalize them for display.

## Negative constraints

pelvis, literal clavicles, human face, mascot proportions, merged shoulder cape, altered
costume, missing/extra/mirrored part, changed harness route, changed pouch side, changed
stitch or oval count, far eye in profile, face on back, perspective, 3/4 view, crop,
extra figure, thick outline, flat vector treatment, pores, fibers, scratches, mottling,
wet gloss, metallic body, translucency, extreme AO, rim/cinematic/volumetric light,
depth of field or bloom.

Absolutely no floor, ground plane, horizon, platform, pedestal, foot ground line,
baseline, guide, grid, cast/contact/drop shadow, reflection, gradient, vignette, text,
labels, arrows, measurements, logo, signature or watermark.

## Controlled Art Bible deviation

This modeling-only derivative deliberately relaxes the source-concept bans on polished
3D-painted presentation, restrained PBR cues, soft planar shading, recessed eye treatment
and tapered jaw language. It does not relax any rule governing Mira's identity, source
lock, nonhuman construction, silhouette, proportion, palette, part count, surface
registration, equipment, originality or final in-game Toon target.
