## Improvements over v04

- Removed full-surface leather grain, pebbled embossing, and cloudy hide patterns.
- Added restrained socket/lid construction to the three locked-image eyes and converted the mouth mark into a closed oral seam.
- Reduced inflated/padded reading of the crescent while preserving its silhouette and thickness envelope.
- Added nonhuman torso tension fields, cartilage insertion, limb taper, and localized joint compression.
- Hands and feet retain the locked three-part counts with clearer pad, flexion, and keratin transitions.
- Both arms remain suspended only from the crescent terminal joints; no human shoulders were introduced.
- No foot baseline or ground line.

## Source discrepancy

The locked pixels visibly show three eyes, while the legacy `reference_manifest.json` describes four. Candidate v09 follows the locked image as visual authority. No source-locked file was edited.

## Limitation

The locked Ori silhouette and large simple color regions are intentionally highly stylized. Further movement toward photorealism beyond v09 would require adding or changing anatomical structures, which would be a design revision rather than a modeling-only material translation.
